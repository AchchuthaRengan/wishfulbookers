# Screen review

Review the primary action, content hierarchy, responsive reflow, keyboard and
touch equivalents, focus order/visibility, screen-reader names, contrast,
reduced motion, and performance. Exercise happy, loading, empty, error, denied,
offline, and narrow-screen states where the screen owns them. Check browser
console and failed network behavior.

Persist desktop/mobile screenshots and accessibility results under the plan's
evidence pack. Record technical verification and founder experience approval as
separate decisions.
