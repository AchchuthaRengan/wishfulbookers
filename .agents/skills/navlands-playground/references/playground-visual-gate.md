# Playground visual gate

Before founder review, render the plan-approved desktop and narrow/mobile
breakpoints and capture happy, loading, empty, recoverable error, origin/source,
comparison, expanded-transition, and true-zero states. Exercise keyboard,
screen-reader semantics, touch alternatives, focus visibility, zoom/reveal, and
reduced motion. Record console/network errors and stable screenshot names in
the evidence pack.

Ask the founder only for unresolved geometry, hierarchy, motion, source-chip
density, `!` treatment, and narrow comparison choices that affect the slice.
Record approval of the rendered experience separately from technical review.
