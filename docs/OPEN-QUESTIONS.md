# Navlands Open Questions

Status: Active and intentionally limited  
Version: 0.3.0  
Date: 2026-07-11

Only unresolved founder/product decisions belong here. Resolved behavior lives
in MOTHER.md or ALGORITHM.md. No implementation may invent an answer.

## Blocking ranking implementation

### OQ-RANK-001 — Numerical factor weights

Which active fit, priority, detail, money, location, freshness, and community
factors receive which exact weights inside each population?

### OQ-RANK-002 — 50-percent normalization

How is a lower-strength factor normalized so `50 percent strength` has one
deterministic mathematical meaning?

### OQ-RANK-003 — Sequential comparison band

How close under priority one must two candidates be before priority two may
decide their order?

### OQ-RANK-004 — Tie and display score

Define tie tolerance, score rounding, and user-visible explanation so match is
never mistaken for success probability.

### OQ-RANK-005 — Vote-factor weight

The Bayesian factor is approved. Its contribution to total ranking remains
unapproved.

### OQ-RANK-006 — Detail score

Define fields, caps, missing penalties, and source/resource freshness without
rewarding unnecessary node count.

### OQ-RANK-007 — Recency ranking

Freshness display/cadence is approved. Decide whether and how stale salary,
course, ladder, or market facts change ranking.

## Blocking AI/ranking semantics

### OQ-AI-001 — Semantic factor rubric

Define the bounded AI-produced responsibility/skill factor fields, scales,
reason codes, and evaluation fixtures.

### OQ-AI-002 — Live-information connectors

Approve exact connectors/APIs and rights rules for live Genie retrieval beyond
the seeded graph.

### OQ-AI-003 — Provider/model selection

Choose through structured-output validity, latency, safety, global availability,
and cost tests. Do not encode provider-specific assumptions before evaluation.

## Blocking paid launch

### OQ-BIZ-001 — Subscription pricing

Set Plus and Pro prices only after valid-path cost tests.

### OQ-BIZ-002 — Included-unit quantities

Five-hour and weekly mechanics are approved provisionally. Confirm or adjust
15u/75u Plus and 30u/150u Pro after unit economics and abuse testing.

### OQ-BIZ-003 — Overage credits

Decide whether paid users may purchase usage credits after included allowance.

### OQ-BIZ-004 — Global payment provider

India uses Razorpay. Select a provider with global country/currency/tax support
before enabling non-India checkout. Until then, non-India paid plans remain
waitlisted behind a country feature flag.
