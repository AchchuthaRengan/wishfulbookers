# Navlands Mother Product Requirements Document

Status: Approved MVP baseline with explicit open questions  
Version: 0.3.0  
Date: 2026-07-11  
Product owner: Founder  
Implementation state: Documentation and architecture

## 1. Product definition

Navlands is a visual career-path product that helps an employee understand
where they can go from their present role, compare paths that people lived with
paths assembled from public sources or generated by AI, and see the roles,
milestones, decisions, time, cost, salary, and possible outcomes between their
starting point and goal.

The primary interface is a simple vertical, zoomable role map. High-level nodes
are roles or role-like career states. Each transition can reveal no more than
three milestone, decision, learning, or guidance subnodes.

Navlands is guidance, not a prediction engine. A match score means fit under
the current inputs and algorithm version; it is never a probability of success.

## 2. MVP goal

For an English-speaking employee anywhere in the world, Navlands should:

1. turn minimal onboarding into a believable map quickly;
2. make adjacent software-career transitions easy to understand;
3. show what each route satisfies and misses;
4. let the user compare real, curated, and AI-created possibilities without
   pretending they have equal origins;
5. let the user fork and adapt a useful route;
6. grow Lived inventory naturally when users choose to publish their paths.

Launch-quality coverage is concentrated on:

- support roles to software roles in product companies; and
- QA roles to development roles.

The product is globally available at launch, but only these corridors receive
the launch coverage guarantee. Salary and learning coverage may vary by
country and must be labelled honestly.

There are no calendar deadlines attached to the development plans. Work moves
by verified milestones and founder-approved gates.

## 3. MVP persona and exclusions

The MVP serves the Employee persona only.

Student, Parent, Company/Employer, verifier, and marketplace behaviors are
excluded. The data model may remain extensible, but no excluded persona screen
or workflow may be implemented.

## 4. Product principles

1. Plain language before algorithm language.
2. Show value before requesting optional publication or additional context.
3. Ask only for information that changes the path.
4. Never fabricate a human history.
5. Never silently relax a user constraint.
6. Preserve origin and fork lineage permanently.
7. Keep ranking deterministic for persisted inputs and versions.
8. Treat votes as community usefulness, never verification.
9. Treat public availability as different from permission to copy.
10. Do not expose a calculator-like configuration surface.

## 5. Core terms and labels

### 5.1 Role node

A high-level career state such as Support Engineer, QA Engineer, Junior
Developer, Senior Developer, Farmer, or Tech Founder.

Start and end roles count toward the path's node total.

### 5.2 Transition subnode

One of no more than three smaller items between adjacent role nodes. It may be
a milestone, decision, course, tutorial, article, skill activity, business
decision, life decision, or transition instruction.

### 5.3 Lived

A path a person publishes as their actual life or work history. It requires no
mandatory proof in MVP and is displayed simply as `Lived`.

Navlands stores its user-declared origin internally but does not add a noisy
"self-reported" badge to every card. It never merges several people into one
invented Lived path.

### 5.4 Curated

A system-created path assembled from public, open, or licensed sources. Its
visible label is:

`Curated — built from public sources`

Curated is an origin label, not verification, proof, endorsement, or a promise
that a person followed the path.

Raw company or government framework pages are not copied into the UI as
standalone reproductions. Navlands may publish an original structured Curated
path built from permitted facts and must show per-node sources plus a Sources
drawer.

### 5.5 AI · Curated

An AI-created path grounded in the Navlands source graph. The label applies
only when every high-level role maps to a canonical known role and at least
half of its transitions have a source-backed basis.

### 5.6 AI · Inferred

An AI-created path containing novel or semantically inferred transitions that
do not meet the AI · Curated threshold. It still requires onboarding context,
structured validation, assumptions, and permanent AI lineage.

### 5.7 Fork

A copy or adaptation of a path. An unchanged manual copy preserves its origin.
If AI changes, adds, removes, or reorders nodes, the result is AI-labelled.

Forks of Curated or AI paths retain immutable origin metadata even if the
origin is later retired. If an origin must be removed, forks retain only a
non-identifying tombstone and independently authored content.

### 5.8 Genie

The AI prompt bar inside Playground. Genie may generate a novel path, adapt a
fork, or refine an AI path. Retrieval and deterministic reranking do not invoke
Genie.

## 6. Canonical user journey

1. Collect the minimum onboarding profile.
2. Ask the user to order two priorities or use the approved fallback.
3. Privately draft the user's existing path.
4. Show the map before asking for optional publication.
5. Use guided highlights to teach reveal, fork, filters, and Genie.
6. Let the user publish the Lived draft at any time; recommendations are not a
   publication prerequisite.
7. Retrieve and rank Lived paths within the Lived population.
8. Retrieve and rank Curated paths within the Curated population.
9. Fill the Lived-side result slots deterministically without scoring Lived
   against Curated.
10. Show exact matches or clearly marked nearest alternatives.
11. Offer one Genie-generated path only after approval and unit reservation.
12. Compare the active AI path beside up to three Lived/Curated results.
13. Let the user save, fork, share, report, vote, refine, or generate again.
14. Keep earlier successful AI generations in `Earlier generations`.

## 7. Onboarding and constraints

### NAV-ONB-001 — Required context

No recommendation or generation occurs without saved onboarding context.

Required initial fields:

- current role;
- actual responsibilities;
- experience range;
- target role;
- current and target salary ranges;
- location;
- relocation or work-location choice when relevant;
- two ordered priorities.

### NAV-ONB-002 — Progressive context

Ask only when it changes the result:

- weekly available hours;
- education/training budget;
- desired timeline;
- explicit hard deadline;
- risk tolerance expressed in plain scenarios;
- employment or family constraints;
- founder-specific context.

### NAV-ONB-003 — Constraint classes

- Target salary: must-match.
- Education budget: preference.
- Location: user chooses must-match or preference.
- Desired timeline: preference by default.
- Explicit `This deadline cannot be exceeded`: must-match.

When no exact salary, location, or hard-deadline match exists, Navlands shows
nearest alternatives with every violation visible and then offers Genie.

### NAV-ONB-004 — Ordered priorities

The user orders two priorities. The first is evaluated before the second under
the sequential model. If skipped, use:

1. highest earning;
2. best onboarding match.

Age is excluded from MVP ranking.

### NAV-ONB-005 — `Not sure`

`Not sure` is permitted for relocation choice, imprecise experience, and
responsibility detail. Experience becomes a wider range; missing responsibility
detail may trigger one material follow-up.

It is not permitted for current role, target role, target salary, or location
because those fields define retrieval and must-match behavior.

## 8. Path structure

### NAV-PATH-001 — Length

- fewer than six high-level nodes is allowed when sufficient;
- six to eight is normal;
- nine or ten requires confirmation;
- more than ten is invalid.

### NAV-PATH-002 — Roles and milestones

Every high-level node is a role or role-like career state. Each transition has
at most three subnodes.

### NAV-PATH-003 — Progressive reveal

Future roles appear partially and reveal through hover, keyboard focus, or
mobile tap so the user can see where a decision leads without opening the
entire graph at once.

### NAV-PATH-004 — Tech Founder endpoint

Tech Founder is reached when the product has at least one active paying
customer. Registration, funding, or incorporation is not required.

Founder personal salary and business revenue possibilities remain separate.

## 9. Origin populations and display

### NAV-ORIGIN-001 — Separate populations

Lived, Curated, and AI are separate ranking populations. No common score is
used to declare one origin better than another.

### NAV-ORIGIN-002 — Lived-side slot allocation

The Lived side retains its permanent name. Curated cards appear in a visually
separated `Curated starting points` section and fill result availability by
rule rather than cross-population score:

| Qualified Lived availability | Up to three default slots |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 3 Curated |
| 1 | 1 Lived + 2 Curated |
| 2–9 | 2 Lived + 1 Curated |
| 10 or more | 3 Lived; Curated becomes fallback/searchable |

### NAV-ORIGIN-003 — Curated retirement

For MVP, `N = 10` complete public Lived paths from ten distinct creators in the
same canonical start-role-family → target-role → country/remote corridor.

Retirement removes Curated from default slots only. It remains searchable,
forkable, usable by AI, and available as a zero-result fallback. Admin may
retire, restore, or pin it manually.

A scoped `Curated starting points` link in the results panel lists
corridor-relevant Curated paths, including retired paths. This is not a global
search or discovery surface.

### NAV-ORIGIN-004 — AI display count

One successful AI path is returned per generation event. One active AI path is
shown beside the Lived side. Refinements version the active path. A new full
generation creates a new path and moves earlier paths to `Earlier generations`.

Invalid generation is not displayed and consumes zero units.

## 10. Retrieval, ranking, and votes

### NAV-RANK-001 — Determinism

Final ordering is deterministic for fixed onboarding, path records, factor
values, vote aggregates, source snapshot, and algorithm version. AI may create
paths or structured semantic values; code produces the final order.

### NAV-RANK-002 — Match score

The visible match score is overall fit, not predicted career success.

### NAV-RANK-003 — Active signals

Upvotes and downvotes affect MVP ranking. Forks, saves, Helped Me, completion,
and outcomes are collected but do not affect MVP ranking.

### NAV-RANK-004 — Vote factor

Eligible votes use:

```text
vote_factor = (U + 2) / (U + D + 4)
```

The no-vote prior is `0.5`. Only the latest live eligible vote per user/path is
counted. Votes are changeable or retractable.

Eligibility is locked at vote-event time and requires:

- completed onboarding;
- voter different from creator.

MVP has no account-age waiting period. Vote controls unlock immediately after
onboarding completes.

The creator's default endorsement may appear in the display total but is
excluded from `U`. The tooltip states that it does not affect ranking.

Ranking weight for `vote_factor` remains open.

### NAV-RANK-005 — Vote abuse baseline

- maximum 20 vote actions per user per UTC day;
- per-IP soft limit of 40 per day with logging/challenge, never a NAT-hostile
  hard block;
- at least 11 votes in a rolling six-hour window with at least 60 percent from
  accounts younger than seven days quarantines those events;
- quarantined votes contribute zero until Admin clears them;
- reciprocal-ring detection begins only after the voter has ten eligible votes
  and is flag-only in MVP.

### NAV-RANK-006 — Reports

Every public Lived, Curated, and AI path has a Report action. Downvotes are
public usefulness signals; reports are private moderation signals.

Report reasons:

- inaccurate;
- outdated;
- spam;
- impersonation/privacy;
- harmful/dangerous;
- copyright/source problem;
- other.

A downvote may offer to submit an inaccurate, outdated, or spam report but
never files one automatically. Reports do not directly change rank in MVP;
moderation actions can quarantine or remove a path.

## 11. No-result behavior

True-zero copy:

> No path matches all your choices yet. See the nearest paths and what they
> miss, or use one Genie wish to create a path around your constraints.

Actions:

- `See nearest paths`;
- `Generate with Genie · 1 wish`;
- `Change filters`.

Every nearest alternative identifies its salary, location, timeline, education
budget, or other mismatch. Nothing is silently relaxed.

When the nearest route violates three constraints, explicitly offer:

- adapt an existing path; or
- generate a full AI path.

## 12. Salary, revenue, and learning

### NAV-MONEY-001 — Ranges and origins

Use annual gross ranges by default. Store location, currency, period, source,
observed/inferred status, and last-checked date.

AI salary copy:

`Estimated range — inferred from available market information, not an actual outcome.`

Curated salary copy:

`Inferred market range — based on available market patterns.`

Where local coverage needs contribution, use the friendly prompt:

`Add a Lived salary path to improve this range for your location.`

### NAV-MONEY-002 — Missing market coverage

Show `!` with the friendly contribution prompt when local information is
missing. Personalized Genie inference requires user approval. Curated inference
requires Admin approval and then appears without a viewer popup. The marker
means information is missing, not that a path is fake, unsafe, or weak.

### NAV-MONEY-003 — Free-source priority

Prefer permitted official sources such as BLS OEWS, Canada Job Bank, UK ONS
ASHE, Jobs and Skills Australia, Eurostat, and India PLFS where sufficiently
granular. Lightcast remains excluded until a suitable paid licence exists.

### NAV-LEARN-001 — Learning resources

Government, accredited, reputable commercial, and AI-discovered resources may
appear. AI-discovered resources require a live URL, identifiable provider,
current availability, relevance, and origin label.

Commercial or affiliate relationships do not influence ranking in MVP. The
future partnership policy remains open.

## 13. Data seeding and rights

### NAV-DATA-001 — Initial scale

Target 50–100 roles, 200–400 transitions, and 20–30 Curated/reference ladders,
with the highest density in the two software launch corridors.

### NAV-DATA-002 — Approved foundation

- O*NET downloadable database as the primary occupational foundation;
- Wikidata structured facts;
- NCS/NCO only after written permission or clearly permitted reuse;
- Progression.fyi only as a discovery index;
- company frameworks only with explicit compatible commercial/open rights;
- no Lightcast until licensed;
- no scraping of LinkedIn profiles, résumés, named personal career histories,
  or social-media biographies without direct consent.

### NAV-DATA-003 — Source snapshots

Full private snapshots are stored only where the licence permits. Otherwise
store URL, retrieval time, content hash, licence record, and permitted
normalized facts. Snapshots are never served as public reproductions.

### NAV-DATA-004 — Provenance and freshness

Every affected node shows source chips and participates in a consolidated
Sources drawer. Imported records pass automated schema, licence, provenance,
duplication, and range checks.

Refresh checks:

- role taxonomy quarterly;
- salary monthly or quarterly according to source cadence;
- company ladders every six months;
- courses monthly;
- Wikidata aliases monthly.

Stale information remains visible with `Last checked` unless withdrawn or
materially unsafe. Full Curated ladders require internal founder/admin review
unless they satisfy NAV-DATA-005; individual facts may pass automated
validation.

### NAV-DATA-005 — Curated auto-publication

An AI-assisted Curated ladder may auto-publish only when every role is
canonical, every transition is backed by permitted source records, all licence,
provenance, schema, duplicate, range, and source-conflict checks pass, and no
rights issue remains unresolved.

AI assembles but does not verify the ladder. Any inferred transition remains a
draft for Admin review. Any inferred salary requires Admin approval before it
appears, including on an otherwise auto-publishable ladder.

## 14. Credits and paid usage

### NAV-CREDIT-001 — Integer units

```text
1 wish = 3 units
full generation or full rerun = 3u
AI refinement = 1u, maximum three per base generated path
AI fork adaptation = 1u
invalid output = 0u
pure retrieval or deterministic reranking = free
manual unchanged fork = free
```

Use reserve → generate → validate → commit, with automatic refund on invalid
output.

### NAV-CREDIT-002 — Provisional plan windows

- Free: 9u one-time launch grant.
- Plus: provisional 15u per five-hour window and 75u weekly.
- Pro: provisional 30u per five-hour window and 150u weekly.

The available included allowance is the lower remaining amount across the
five-hour and weekly windows. Unused included units do not accumulate. Users
see usage bars and exact reset times.

Limits are acceptable for MVP specification but remain provisional until
model-cost tests and subscription prices validate unit economics.

### NAV-CREDIT-003 — Paid rollout

Paid plans launch in India through Razorpay. Navlands remains globally
available, but non-India paid subscriptions stay behind a country feature flag
and waiting list until a globally suitable payment provider is approved.

Plus → Pro upgrades take effect immediately with the billing provider's normal
prorated charge or credit.

## 15. Publication, privacy, deletion, and completion

### NAV-TRUST-001 — Publication

Lived publication is explicit. Private drafts and transition events are private
by default. Proof, verifier roles, Human Verified, and AI Verified are excluded.

### NAV-TRUST-002 — Tombstone deletion

On deletion, remove identity, descriptions, private data, and authored content;
retain only a non-identifying tombstone and safe lineage identifiers. Forks show
`Original path removed`. Copied identifying content is removed.

### NAV-TRUST-003 — Consent and privacy preferences

Signup presents a clear service-data notice. Optional engine-improvement use
requires a separate opt-in. Public-path consent is requested at publication,
not signup. Users can withdraw optional consent and manage privacy preferences
from Profile. See PRIVACY.md.

### NAV-TRUST-004 — Notifications

In-app notifications may cover missing-information requests, moderation
outcomes, credit refunds, opted-in usage resets, source withdrawal for owned
forks, forks of the user's public path, grouped upvote activity, and public-fork
node changes originating from the user's path.

Downvote and report notifications never reveal the actor. Saves remain private
and create no creator notification. Email/push is opt-in and limited to
important events. No streaks, generic digests, or re-engagement nudges.

### NAV-COMP-001 — No progress tracking

There is no MVP progress dashboard or percentage. Selecting a later role may
ask whether the prior role was completed and store a private event.

Employment completion means entering the target role in a company. Tech
Founder completion means the product has at least one active paying customer.
Completion is collection-only for MVP ranking.

## 16. MVP surfaces

Included:

1. Landing and employee onboarding.
2. Minimal private profile and private draft path.
3. Playground vertical role graph and Genie.
4. Lived, Curated, and AI result comparison.
5. Filters and two ordered priorities.
6. Progressive reveal and guided highlights.
7. Save, share, fork, vote, report, and publish.
8. Minimal public path/profile artifact.
9. Missing-information notifications and Sources drawer.
10. Earlier AI generations.
11. Internal source, moderation, and content administration.

Excluded:

- Student, Parent, and Company personas;
- verifier roles, Human Verified, and AI Verified;
- News Feed and generic how-to timelines;
- Trending, Hot, and Rising discovery;
- rich playful portfolio or résumé replacement;
- celebrity What-If;
- job marketplace;
- progress dashboard;
- automatic behavioral learning or weight changes.

## 17. Technology direction

- Next.js App Router and strict TypeScript;
- Supabase Auth, Postgres, Row Level Security, Realtime where justified, and
  Storage;
- shadcn-based UI;
- React Flow isolated in an accessible graph client;
- structured AI output with runtime validation;
- versioned roles, paths, prompts, algorithms, sources, votes, and credits;
- background ingestion and recomputation jobs;
- email/password and Google OAuth;
- free GitHub Actions CI while within included quota, with a zero-spend ceiling;
- Cloudflare Workers/OpenNext compatibility validation for free commercial CD;
- local full validation remains authoritative if hosted CI/CD is unavailable.

See ARCHITECTURE.md, DATA-MODEL.md, AI-CONTRACTS.md, and SECURITY.md.

## 18. Launch validation

Cold-start content is ready when:

- at least 90 percent of the fixed launch-corridor queries return one usable
  source-grounded route without AI failure; and
- at least 70 percent return a top result satisfying the constraint thresholds
  associated with the fixture's two ordered priorities.

Maintain at least 100 fixed onboarding/goal fixtures, including difficult
transitions such as Farmer → Tech Founder.

Primary validation events include onboarding completion, first believable
path, reveal, compare, save, fork, share, Genie use, report rate, return use,
and optional Lived publication. Exact product KPI targets remain in
ANALYTICS.md and OPEN-QUESTIONS.md.

## 19. Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not recommend without onboarding.
2. Do not fabricate or merge human histories.
3. Do not modify an original Lived path.
4. Do not rank origin populations against one another.
5. Do not call match a success probability.
6. Do not present inferred money as actual outcome.
7. Do not silently relax must-match constraints.
8. Do not use public availability as copying permission.
9. Do not charge for deterministic reranking or invalid AI output.
10. Do not implement excluded personas, verification, or progress tracking.
11. Do not change product behavior without founder approval.

## 20. Remaining decisions

The unresolved items are intentionally limited to OPEN-QUESTIONS.md, including
ranking weights, sequential comparison bands, detail scoring, vote-factor
weight, exact paid pricing/unit economics, global payment-provider selection,
final salary-source registry, partnership neutrality rules, visual prototypes,
and launch KPI targets.
